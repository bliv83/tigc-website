About

A strategist who reads the relationship from both sides.

Dr. Kelly Alexander

Coming from a deep conviction that Africa and Europe work better together, Dr. Kelly Alexander advises companies and leaders on how to navigate both continents. She helps them understand each other and build strategies that work in both places. She advises on strategy, policy, and sustainability — the practical side of making the two continents work together.

She holds a PhD in development sociology and brings academic rigour to commercial conversations without slipping into academic language. As President and Founder of Meridian17, she advises clients on both sides of the Africa-Europe relationship — African companies and institutions engaging European markets, policy, and capital; European companies operating in or with Africa — runs leadership workshops that surface the tensions most firms work around, and publishes independent research through the TIGC think tank.

She is a dual South African and Dutch national, faculty at the Gordon Institute of Business Science (GIBS) in Johannesburg, and a visiting lecturer at several European and African business schools. Past and current clients include Ballast Nedam International, the Energy Intensive Users Group (EIUG), and participants in Harvard's inter-ministerial leadership programme.

She lives between Johannesburg and the Netherlands — not as a generalist on two regions, but as someone who has spent a career figuring out why Europe and Africa so often talk past each other, and what it takes from either side of the table to get them to stop.

Approach

How I work

"Clearer, not simpler"

The problems at this interface don't collapse into a tidy framework — and they don't get tidier depending on which capital you're sitting in. My job is to help you think more clearly about them, not to pretend they are neater than they are.

"Rigour without the jargon"

A PhD teaches you when the evidence holds and when it doesn't. It doesn't require you to speak like a journal paper — in English, Afrikaans, Dutch, or anything else.

"Senior rooms only"

I work with leadership teams and senior advisors. The value I add is synthesis and judgement across a relationship most people only see one end of — not running a research operation or staffing a project.
