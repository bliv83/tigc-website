Immersion programmes

Experience Africa and Europe from the inside.

Reading about another continent only takes you so far. I organise and host immersion programmes that take you into the cultural context and into the rooms where doing business between Africa and Europe really happens. Whatever your challenge, I build a programme around it.

What an immersion is

Time spent where it really happens.

A single day, a few days, sometimes longer, spent on the other continent. You step into boardrooms, offices, ministries, and universities. You meet companies doing the work and policy experts who can speak to on-the-ground reality. Every visit has a purpose you set out with.

You come home with a clearer picture of how business works on the ground, and relationships you can keep building on.

Who it's for

Built for different needs.

A company entering a new market, or rethinking the one it is already in.
A group of organisations looking for a shared experience across borders.
An individual executive preparing for a new role or a new challenge.
A team wanting to truly understand a partner continent.

Why this works

A network that opens doors.

I work with a network across both continents: business leaders, think tanks, policy makers, and business school faculty. This is what makes each programme possible, and what makes each one different. The doors I open are ones that would be hard to open on your own.

How it works

Tailored from the first conversation.

We start with a conversation about what you want to understand or solve. I design a programme around that. I host you throughout, guide the conversations, and help you make sense of what you are seeing.

You leave with a sharper view of the continent you came to understand, and the confidence to act on it.

High-level example of a 3-day programme

First step
Discovery conversation
We start with a conversation about what you want to understand or solve. In this 90-minute call, I learn what your challenge is, what you're worried about, and what success looks like for you. This conversation shapes everything that comes next.
Before departure
Drafting and tuning your programme
Based on our conversation, I design a programme around your needs using my network across both continents. We identify the business leaders, operators, and decision-makers you need to meet. You'll understand how business actually works in this context, dive into the cultural reality, and have time to enjoy the people and the place.
Day 1
On the ground meetings
You step into the rooms where business actually happens. You meet companies operating in your space, understand how they work on the ground, and get a real sense of the landscape. Every meeting has a purpose. You start seeing patterns.
Day 2
Understanding policy and decision-makers
You meet the people who shape the operating environment. Government officials, regulators, industry associations, think tanks. You understand how decisions get made, what the constraints are, and who actually matters when something needs to happen.
Day 3
Deepening connections and clarity
We follow up on the questions that emerged from the first two days. You sit in conversations where the frank answers come out. You build relationships that will last. By the end, the picture is sharper.
After
Brief document with contacts and insights
Within a week, you get a document naming everyone you met, their roles, what you discussed, and where to go next. It's not a report. It's a map: who matters, how they think, and what comes next.
