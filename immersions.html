Immersion programs

Experience Africa and Europe from the inside.

Reading about another continent only takes you so far. I organise and host immersion programs that take you into the cultural context and into the rooms where doing business between Africa and Europe really happens. Whatever your challenge, I build a program around it.

What an immersion is

Time spent where it really happens.

A single day, a few days, sometimes longer, spent on the other continent. You step into boardrooms, factories, ministries, and universities. You meet companies doing the work and policy experts who can speak to on-the-ground reality. Every visit has a purpose you set out with.

You come home with a clearer picture of how business works on the ground, and relationships you can keep building on.

Who it's for

Built for different needs.

A company entering a new market, or rethinking the one it is already in.
A group of organisations looking for a shared experience across borders.
An individual executive preparing for a new role or a new challenge.
A team wanting to truly understand a partner continent.

Why this works

A network that opens doors.

I work with a network across both continents: business leaders, think tanks, policy makers, and business school faculty. This is what makes each program possible, and what makes each one different. The doors I open are ones that would be hard to open on your own.

How it works

Tailored from the first conversation.

We start with a conversation about what you want to understand or solve. I design a program around that. I host you throughout, guide the conversations, and help you make sense of what you are seeing.

You leave with a sharper view of the continent you came to understand, and the confidence to act on it.
