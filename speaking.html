Speaking & Facilitation

A steadier hand on difficult conversations.

I speak and facilitate at senior level — at conferences, executive forums, and private leadership events — on the strategy and social dimensions of the Africa-Europe relationship. I also chair panels and convene expert dialogues for organisations working on sustainability and Africa-facing strategy.

Signature talk

A keynote that reframes the conversation

"Why the frameworks aren't working: sustainability strategy between Europe and Africa."

A keynote or panel contribution for audiences sitting between European sustainability commitments and African operating realities — and noticing the gap is wider than the reporting suggests. Built on independent research, two decades of advisory work, and a lot of time in the classroom with senior leaders from both sides.
