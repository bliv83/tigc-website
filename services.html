What I offer

Three ways we can work together.

01

Workshop

Format
Half-day or full-day facilitated workshop
For
Senior leadership teams in European companies with African operations; DFI investee companies; organisations navigating just transition, social licence, or community impact

Sustainability Complexity Workshops

A facilitated session that pulls the real tensions in your sustainability commitments into the open — between stakeholders, operations, and what leadership has actually signed up to — and gives your team a way to work with them rather than around them.

The method is drawn from postgraduate executive education: MBA-level case teaching, adapted for a boardroom. The point is to create the conditions for an honest strategic conversation, which most large-consultancy workshops are structurally unable to deliver.

Participants leave with sharper framing of the actual problem. Not a slide deck of generic recommendations.

Pricing

From €4,000 (half-day)  |  €7,000–12,000 (full day)  |  Multi-session programmes available on request

02

Retainer

Format
4–6 hours per month, ongoing engagement
For
Senior leaders or small teams who need a trusted outside perspective on Africa-facing strategy, sustainability positioning, or policy navigation

Retainer Advisory

A monthly retainer gives you a senior outside view on call — without the overhead of a large firm. We meet regularly. You get honest analysis, a second opinion when you need one, and someone who already knows your context when a decision lands on your desk.

The most useful advisory relationships start before the crisis, when there is still room to shape direction rather than manage fallout.

This is not project consulting. It is a sustained relationship with someone who knows your situation, speaks your language, and will tell you what she actually thinks.

Pricing

From €1,500–2,500/month depending on scope and time commitment

03

Research

Format
Project-based, delivered through TIGC
For
Organisations, associations, and multilaterals needing high-quality, strategically framed research on Africa-Europe dynamics

Strategic Research & Position Papers

I design and direct research. I don't run fieldwork or code transcripts. The output is a position paper or strategic brief that a senior audience can actually read end to end.

A 20-page argument, not a 200-page report. Clear on what it claims, honest about what it can't yet know, and written to be useful to someone making a real decision.

Recent and current topics: Africa-Europe relations, sustainability transitions, social impact measurement, just transition policy, and the implications of the EU's CBAM for African producers and exporters.

Pricing

From €5,000 (short brief)  |  €10,000–20,000 (full position paper)
