What I offer

Four ways we can work together.

01

Workshop

Format
Half-day or full-day facilitated workshop
For
Senior leadership teams working across the Africa-Europe line — European companies with African operations, African companies entering European markets or engaging European regulators and capital, DFI investees and investors, and organisations working on just transition, social licence, or community impact.

Sustainability Complexity Workshops

A facilitated session where the real tensions in your strategy come out. Stakeholders, operations, regulators, your leadership commitments. Your team learns to work with these tensions, not around them.

We use case-method teaching from MBA programmes, adapted for the boardroom. You get an honest conversation, not a presentation. Most big consulting firms can't deliver this, especially when two continents see the same problem completely differently.

You leave with sharper framing of the actual problem. Not a slide deck of generic recommendations.

Enquire about a workshop
02

Retainer

Format
4–6 hours per month, ongoing engagement
For
Senior leaders and small teams working either direction of the Africa-Europe relationship — shaping Africa strategy from Europe, or shaping European strategy, policy engagement, or capital raising from Africa.

Retainer Advisory

A monthly standing relationship. You get an outside view without hiring a big firm. We meet regularly. Honest analysis. A second opinion when you need one. Someone who already understands the other side.

The best advisory relationships start before the crisis. When there's still time to shape direction, not just manage fallout.

This is not project work. It's a sustained relationship. I know your situation. I read both continents. I'll tell you what I actually think.

Discuss a retainer
03

Research

Format
Project-based, delivered through TIGC
For
Companies, associations, regulators, and multilateral organisations — on either continent — that need high-quality, strategically framed research on Africa-Europe dynamics.

Strategic Research & Position Papers

I design and direct research. I don't do fieldwork or sit with data. The output is a brief your senior team can actually read. Written for decision-makers, not academics.

20 pages, not 200. Clear on what we know. Honest about what we don't. Written for someone making a decision Monday morning.

Current topics: Africa-Europe trade and strategy. Sustainability transitions. Social impact measurement. Just transition policy. CBAM and what it means for both sides.

Discuss a research brief
04

Immersion

Format
Single or multi-day tailored programme, on the ground
For
Companies entering a new market, groups of organisations wanting a shared experience, executives preparing for a new role, or individuals who want to understand a continent from the inside.

Immersions

You go to the places and rooms where business actually happens. Meet companies on the ground. Talk to policy experts. People who speak honestly. You experience the cultural context, not read about it.

I design it around your question. My network opens doors you'd struggle to open alone. Business leaders, think tanks, policy makers, faculty. I host you throughout. I help you make sense of what you see.

No two immersions are the same, because no two challenges are the same.
