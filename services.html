What I offer

Four ways we can work together.

01

Workshop

Format
Half-day or full-day facilitated workshop
For
Senior leadership teams working across the Africa-Europe line — European companies with African operations, African companies entering European markets or engaging European regulators and capital, DFI investees and investors, and organisations working on just transition, social licence, or community impact.

Sustainability Complexity Workshops

A facilitated session that pulls the real tensions in your strategy into the open — between stakeholders, operations, regulators, and what leadership has actually signed up to — and gives your team a way to work with them rather than around them.

The method is drawn from postgraduate executive education: MBA-level case teaching, adapted for a boardroom. It creates the conditions for an honest strategic conversation, which most large-consultancy workshops are structurally unable to deliver — particularly when the conversation involves two continents reading the same situation very differently.

Participants leave with sharper framing of the actual problem. Not a slide deck of generic recommendations.

Enquire about a workshop
02

Retainer

Format
4–6 hours per month, ongoing engagement
For
Senior leaders and small teams working either direction of the Africa-Europe relationship — shaping Africa strategy from Europe, or shaping European strategy, policy engagement, or capital raising from Africa.

Retainer Advisory

A monthly retainer gives you a senior outside view on call — without the overhead of a large firm. We meet regularly. You get honest analysis, a second opinion when you need one, and someone who already understands the context on the other side of the relationship when a decision lands on your desk.

The most useful advisory relationships start before the crisis, when there is still room to shape direction rather than manage fallout.

This is not project consulting. It is a sustained relationship with someone who knows your situation, reads both continents fluently, and will tell you what she actually thinks.

Discuss a retainer
03

Research

Format
Project-based, delivered through TIGC
For
Companies, associations, regulators, and multilateral organisations — on either continent — that need high-quality, strategically framed research on Africa-Europe dynamics.

Strategic Research & Position Papers

I design and direct research. I don't run fieldwork or code transcripts. The output is a position paper or strategic brief that a senior audience can actually read end to end, whether that audience sits in Brussels, Pretoria, Abidjan, or Amsterdam.

A 20-page argument, not a 200-page report. Clear on what it claims, honest about what it can't yet know, and written to be useful to someone making a real decision.

Recent and current topics: Africa-Europe strategic and trade relations, sustainability transitions, social impact measurement, just transition policy, and the implications of the EU's CBAM — both for European importers and for African producers and exporters trying to stay in the market.

Discuss a research brief
04

Immersion

Format
Single or multi-day tailored programme, on the ground
For
Companies entering a new market, groups of organisations wanting a shared experience, executives preparing for a new role, or individuals who want to understand a continent from the inside.

Immersions

An immersion takes you to the places, rooms, and conversations where doing business between Africa and Europe actually happens. You meet companies on the ground, policy experts, and people who can speak honestly about what it takes to succeed. You dive into the cultural context rather than reading about it.

I design each programme around the question you come with. Through my network of business leaders, think tanks, policy makers, and business school faculty, I open doors that would be hard to open on your own. I host you throughout and help you make sense of what you are seeing.

No two immersions are the same, because no two challenges are the same.
